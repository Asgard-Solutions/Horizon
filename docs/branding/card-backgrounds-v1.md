# Original media-card backgrounds v1

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for Horizon Media Center. These are decorative navigation backgrounds, independent of library records and media artwork. The Home page retains its existing background. The supplied logos are unchanged.

- Movies: `frontend/src/assets/horizon-movies-card-background-v1.png`, 2048×768 PNG, warm cinema projector and film reel.
- TV Shows: `frontend/src/assets/horizon-tv-card-background-v1.png`, 2050×767 PNG, cool television-studio display silhouettes.

The original generated files are preserved. Card CSS adds text-protection overlays and uses right-aligned cropping to retain the subject on smaller screens. No titles, posters, program thumbnails, UI or lettering are baked into either asset.

Verification: the production build passed. Actual app inspection at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 390×844 confirmed separate loaded background assets and readable labels. Keyboard focus moved from Movies to TV Shows while retaining its TV-specific image. At 390×844, neither label overlaps its icon and Settings remains within the viewport. Screenshots are in `docs/verification/cinematic-controls/home-original-card-backgrounds-1920x1080.jpg` and `home-original-card-backgrounds-compact.jpg`.

## Movies prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original raster background only for the Movies selection card in Horizon Media Center, not a picture of a user interface. Create a single very wide landscape composition, approximately 2560x960 (8:3). A premium cinematic projection-booth atmosphere: on the far right, a partially cropped real 35mm cinema projector lens and film-reel silhouette in charcoal metal, subtle warm amber reflections, a soft diagonal projector beam through fine haze and a few defocused golden optical glints. The mood is elegant, rich and cinematic, with realistic materials and restrained lighting. The left 60 percent must remain very dark midnight navy with soft low-contrast atmosphere and generous clean space for readable white interface labels. Main photographic detail occupies the right half and remains readable when cropped into an 852x328 card. Keep highlights soft rather than white or overexposed. Palette: near-black navy, muted bronze/amber, tiny teal reflections only in the shadows. No people, no words, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no buttons, no border, no movie posters, no characters, no sample movie artwork, no glowing horizontal horizon or landscape. Opaque finished background, not transparent.

## TV Shows prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original raster background only for the TV Shows selection card in Horizon Media Center, not a picture of a user interface. Create a single very wide landscape composition, approximately 2560x960 (8:3). A premium after-hours television-production studio atmosphere: on the right half, elegant overlapping physical widescreen display silhouettes in dark glass, turned off with no pictures or interface, soft teal and cool blue vertical studio-light reflections, subtle optical depth and gentle atmospheric haze. An understated broadcast-studio feeling, precise and modern, visually distinct from a warm cinema projector. The left 60 percent must remain very dark midnight navy with soft low-contrast atmosphere and generous clean space for readable white interface labels. Main detail occupies the right half and remains readable when cropped into an 852x328 card. Keep highlights soft and localized. Palette: near-black navy, deep blue, restrained luminous teal; almost no gold. No people, no words, no lettering, no channel names, no logos, no watermark, no buttons, no border, no program thumbnails, no posters, no characters, no sample TV artwork, no cinema projector, no glowing horizontal horizon or landscape. Opaque finished background, not transparent.
